reflector has been deployed.

It watches Secrets, ConfigMaps and Namespaces cluster-wide and mirrors resources
according to the reflector.v1.k8s.emberstack.com annotations — fully compatible
with the original emberstack/kubernetes-reflector.

Health endpoints (container port 8080):
  - liveness:  /health/live
  - readiness: /health/ready

Mark a source Secret/ConfigMap so it can be reflected:

  metadata:
    annotations:
      reflector.v1.k8s.emberstack.com/reflection-allowed: "true"
      reflector.v1.k8s.emberstack.com/reflection-allowed-namespaces: "team-.*"
      reflector.v1.k8s.emberstack.com/reflection-auto-enabled: "true"
      reflector.v1.k8s.emberstack.com/reflection-auto-namespaces: "team-.*"

Check the controller logs:

  kubectl -n {{ include "reflector.namespace" . }} logs deploy/{{ include "reflector.fullname" . }}
